Welcome to Phulera Marbles

Established in 1994, Phulera Marbles Pvt. Ltd. has grown steadily over the last three decades to become a trusted name in the natural stone industry in India. With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we are proud to be one of the leading manufacturers and exporters of premium marble and granite from Rajasthan.From humble beginnings, our journey has been marked by consistent development, expansion, and modernization. Today, we stand as a symbol of trust, offering a wide array of marble and granite products in various finishes, colors, and textures, directly sourced from the quarries and processed in our advanced facilities.

With extensive warehouse and processing capabilities, Phulera Marbles is known for its superior-quality natural stones that are widely used in residential, commercial, and hospitality projects around the globe. Whether it's elegant countertops, designer tabletops, or luxury flooring, our products are crafted to meet global standards and the aesthetic expectations of our clients. Our core strength lies in understanding the unique needs of every customer and delivering tailor-made solutions that exceed expectations. Our customer-centric approach, powered by a skilled and experienced team, ensures timely production, strict quality control, seamless packaging, and efficient delivery.

We specialize in Rajasthan’s high-grade granite and marble, which are known for their strength, beauty, and versatility. These materials are ideal for flooring, cladding, interior design, and architectural use. Each slab and tile is carefully processed to maintain the natural charm of the stone while enhancing its durability and finish.

Manufacturing process

Quarrying

Carefully extracting premium granite and marble blocks from selected quarries.

Cutting & Shaping

Precision cutting into slabs and customized dimensions using advanced machinery.

Polishing & Finishing

Enhancing natural beauty with finishes like glossy, matte, flamed, or leathered.

Quality Check&Packaging

Rigorous inspection and secure packing to ensure flawless delivery worldwide
